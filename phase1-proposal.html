Phase 1 / Foundation only

RJL Live Gate — Revised Phase 1 (Foundation) Proposal

This revision is limited to Phase 1 (Foundation) scope only and removes future-enhancement-led items from the committed delivery scope unless separately approved later.

The purpose of Phase 1 is to deliver only those works, devices, software and services that have a current operational, safety or compliance requirement at the site.

Future compatibility and excluded enhancement scope

Where future compatibility can be achieved through sensible design with little or no additional cost, that is beneficial. However, this revision excludes equipment, infrastructure or software included primarily for a possible future enhancement rather than a current Phase 1 requirement.

Phase 1 does not include dedicated AI inspection, AI damage comparison, ANPR, thermal imaging, GPS tracking or other future enhancement capability unless separately approved in a later phase.

Executive decision requested

Approval is sought to progress Phase 1 measured survey, detailed design and stakeholder confirmation only.

  • No approval is requested at this stage for future enhancement functionality.
  • No approval is requested at this stage for procurement or construction beyond the Phase 1 scope confirmed through detailed design.
  • At completion of this stage, Hawk should receive the measured survey outcomes, the detailed design package, the confirmed Phase 1 scope split, the clarified responsibility model and the commercial breakdown needed for a further implementation decision.
  • Progress through this stage does not in itself create an obligation for Hawk to award later implementation works to RJL.

Scope split

Phase 1 (Foundation) — Required

  • Measured survey and detailed design for the current gate-control requirement.
  • Gate operating workflow, status logic and exception handling for current operational use.
  • Current-condition image capture only where there is a present operational, evidentiary or compliance requirement.
  • Civil, electrical, communications and mounting works only where required for approved Phase 1 devices.
  • Testing, commissioning, training and handover for the approved Phase 1 system.

Phase 1 (Foundation) — Optional

  • Optional items may be included only where they support a current Phase 1 operational, safety or compliance need.
  • Optional items must be identified separately from required scope and costed separately for decision.
  • Any optional item remains subject to detailed design confirmation.

Future enhancement — Not included

  • ANPR.
  • AI damage comparison or advanced vehicle-condition analytics.
  • Dedicated AI inspection hardware.
  • Thermal imaging.
  • GPS or other enhancement-led technology not required for current Phase 1 operation.

Vehicle-condition capture

Phase 1 may include basic image capture to create a current operational record where required. Phase 1 does not include automated AI comparison, automated damage identification or dedicated AI infrastructure.

The current Phase 1 cameras may provide imagery that could later be assessed for enhancement analytics, but that future capability is not committed in this scope and may still require a separate dedicated inspection solution.

Amber workflow

An Amber outcome records and escalates the exception to the relevant local operations team, but does not itself hold the vehicle at the gate. Subject to the local safety controls being satisfied, vehicle movement continues while the exception is managed separately.

Programme position

This revision identifies the Phase 1 delivery sequence but does not commit final durations. The executive issue should either include an indicative programme covering detailed design, procurement, civil/electrical works, installation, configuration, commissioning and handover, or explicitly state that the programme will be issued after measured survey and detailed design.

  • Measured survey and stakeholder confirmation
  • Detailed design and engineering coordination
  • Procurement of approved Phase 1 items
  • Civil and electrical works
  • Installation and configuration
  • Testing, commissioning and handover
